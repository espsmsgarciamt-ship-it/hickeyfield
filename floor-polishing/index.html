Pricing Basis

  • Existing slab + 40mm topping + polishing
  • Minimum concrete strength: 3,500 PSI (preferred 4,000 PSI)
  • Complete system rate: ₱1,800/sqm minimum
  • Polishing only: rate is editable (₱900/sqm placeholder, to be confirmed)
  • Mobilization: ₱100,000 lump sum
  • Example: 100 sqm × ₱1,800 = ₱180,000 + ₱100,000 = ₱280,000
