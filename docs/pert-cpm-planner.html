Three-point estimates · critical path · Monte Carlo · S-curves

PERT / CPM planner

Enter each activity with an optimistic, most likely and pessimistic duration and the activities it waits for. The planner finds the critical path, the float on every activity, the chance of meeting your deadline, and both S-curves. It loads with a sample release project; replace it with your own. Everything runs in your browser and is saved on this device only.

S-curve data as CSV

Method: CPM forward and backward pass with finish-to-start links; PERT mean and variance per activity; Monte Carlo samples each activity from a PERT-Beta distribution and recomputes the longest path every run. Classical PERT uses the critical path only (when paths tie, the one with the largest variance). Same method as scripts/pert_cpm.py in the Hickeyfield repo.
